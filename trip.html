How the comparison works

Search text is sent to Photon (komoot). Selected coordinates are sent to the routing and weather services. Saved trip settings stay in this browser.

One road route is calculated by the FOSSGIS OSRM service using OpenStreetMap. Travel times do not include live traffic, closures, or weather delays. Check the route before using it. Break time is spread across the drive; the selected buffer increases driving time.

Checkpoints are spaced about 30 minutes of estimated driving apart. GFS and ECMWF hourly forecasts are matched to each checkpoint’s arrival time, with a ±1 hour window. The map and flags show conditions across that window; temperature columns show the nearest forecast hour. Both models are requested across the same locations. Actual model coverage may be shorter than 16 days.

Only departures with complete temperature, precipitation, snowfall, weather-code, and gust data from both models, plus GFS visibility at every checkpoint/window hour, are ranked. Other departures remain visible and cannot be treated as low concern. Ranking uses the highest concern level, then the number of checkpoints with stronger concerns, caution, and any flags. This is a screening rule, not a calibrated probability of an accident or road ice.

Flags include forecast snow, freezing precipitation, wet weather at 2°C or below, gusts of 50 km/h or more, visibility below 3 km, thunderstorms, and heavier precipitation of at least 3 mm/h. Temperatures do not measure pavement temperature; treatment, prior snowfall, and local conditions can change road conditions. A few-mile route deviation or timing delay can change your exposure.

Review official forecasts and road reports before traveling: National Weather Service · Illinois roads · Georgia 511.
